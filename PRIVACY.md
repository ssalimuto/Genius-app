# Privacy Policy: Ssalimuto AI and Genius AI

Last updated: 12 November 2026. Developer: Ssali Muto, Uganda (ssalimuto@gmail.com).

- **Your account stays on your phone.** Your account, secret code (stored only as a protected hash), memory and projects are saved on your device. We don't run a server that collects them.
- **AI answers.** When you ask something, your message is sent to free public AI services (for example Pollinations and LLM7) to produce the answer, and pages you ask about are fetched from the web (for example Wikipedia and DuckDuckGo). Don't type passwords, PINs or bank numbers into chat.
- **Optional permissions.** Reading messages, the screen or SMS only works if you turn it on in Android settings, and the information is used on your phone to answer you.
- **No ads, no selling data, no payments.** The apps are free.
- **Updates.** The apps download updates from GitHub.
- **Children.** The apps aren't designed to collect personal information from children.
- **Contact.** Email ssalimuto@gmail.com to ask a question or have anything removed.
